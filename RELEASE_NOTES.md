# OmniPlay & OmniQualizer v0.17.0

## What's New

### 🎵 Floating Quick Equalizer
- **Instant Audio Control**: Tap the Equalizer button anywhere in OmniPlay to bring up the Quick Equalizer popup without interrupting playback or navigating away from your music.
- **Pure Transparent Overlay**: Enjoy a seamless popup experience that floats cleanly over your album art and queue with zero background dimming or dark overlays.
- **On-the-Fly Adjustments**: Fine-tune your 10-band equalizer sliders, boost bass with ClearBass, switch sound presets, or reset everything to flat in seconds.
- **Quick Expand**: Tap the full-screen icon whenever you want to open the full OmniQualizer dashboard.

### ⚡ Seamless OmniPlay & OmniQualizer Integration
- **Unified Ecosystem**: OmniPlay and OmniQualizer now link automatically. OmniPlay delegates audio processing directly to OmniQualizer for high-precision DSP audio effects.
- **Long-Press Shortcut**: Long-press the Equalizer button on the player bar or access the Equalizer option in Settings to launch OmniQualizer directly.

### 📱 Active Session Detector Improvements
- **Real App Names & Icons**: Active audio sessions now clearly show the actual app name (e.g., YouTube, Spotify, Chrome) along with clean, high-resolution app icons.
- **Clean Interface**: Empty placeholder text is hidden when no audio is playing, and notification session counters stay neat and clutter-free.
- **Streamlined Settings**: Active audio sessions have moved to Settings for a cleaner, more focused main screen.

### 🎨 Visual & Design Consistency
- **Shared Material 3 Expressive Styling**: Harmonized colors, shapes, and responsive layouts across both OmniPlay and OmniQualizer for a consistent, premium feel.
- **Polished Controls**: Refined spacing on Intelligent Equalizer chips and cleaner switch toggles.
